Projects

Featured engineering project

SHER CAR

Sustainable Energy Vehicle

A sustainable vehicle concept designed to draw on multiple forms of energy at once — reducing dependence on any single source and pointing toward more resilient personal transport.

🏆 1st Place — Senior Level, National Science Fair, Jamal Mohamed College, Tiruchirappalli
Heat Energy Thermal energy capture
Solar Energy Photovoltaic charging
Electrical Energy Battery-driven propulsion
Problem

Conventional vehicles rely on a single energy source, limiting efficiency and sustainability, especially at small scale.

Solution

SHER CAR combines heat, solar, and electrical energy inputs into one system, so the vehicle can draw on whichever source is most available.

Technologies / Concepts
Thermoelectric conversionPhotovoltaic cellsBattery managementHybrid energy systems
